# Outreach templates — first 10 users

Goal: 10 people run a report on their own product. Method: concierge reports,
outcome-first framing, manual reply drafts. 25-30 sends ≈ 10 runs.

Rules: personalize every send with one specific detail. No copy-paste blasts.
No "check out my tool". No asking for upvotes, ever.

---

## 1. First-touch DM — outcome-first (default)

Placeholders: [product], [founder name], [N], [subreddit], [thread title / pain],
[share link]

> Hey [founder name] — saw your launch of [product] on [Show HN / r/SideProject].
> Congrats on shipping.
>
> I research where products' future users already talk online. I ran [product]
> through my process and found [N] live Reddit threads where people describe the
> exact problem you solve — including one in r/[subreddit]: "[thread title]"
> ([score] points, [comments] comments).
>
> Full report, free, no signup: [share link]
>
> If useful, I can also draft replies for the 2-3 best threads — the part most
> founders get stuck on. Just say the word.

Why it works: leads with their outcome (warm threads), cites one specific
thread as proof, gives value before asking anything, reply draft = the manual
layer the tool doesn't do yet.

### Variants

**HN reply (public, on their Show HN thread):**
Drop the "saw your launch" line. Start: "Nice — congrats on shipping. I ran
[product] through a research process that finds where your users already talk…"
Keep rest identical. Public replies must still be 100% value — no hint of pitch.

**Reddit DM:**
Reddit hates marketers harder. Add one line of genuine reaction to their product
first ("the [specific feature] is smart because [reason]"). Then template.

**Email (if in their HN profile / site):**
Subject: `[N] threads where [product]'s users already talk`
Body: same template, trimmed to 4 sentences.

---

## 2. Responder follow-up — deliver the manual layer

When someone replies positively, send within 2h:

> Great — here are replies drafted for your 3 best threads: [link or inline].
> They're written to help first and mention [product] only where it genuinely
> fits — that's what survives on Reddit/HN. Edit freely, your voice matters
> more than mine.
>
> One question so I make this actually useful: what would make you come back
> and run this again next week?

Log every answer to the last question. That list = roadmap + testimonials.

---

## 3. Non-responder nudge (send once, 3-4 days later)

> Quick one — did the report land? The r/[subreddit] thread from it is still
> active ([comments] comments now), worth a reply while it's warm.

One nudge max. Silence after that = move on.

---

## 4. Pay probe (week 2, after free delivery)

Only after they've received report + drafts and responded well:

> Curious: if I did this for you every week — fresh threads found, replies
> drafted, you just review and post — would that be worth $50/mo? Not charging
> yet, just calibrating what to build.

Track: yes / maybe / no + reasons. 5+ "yes" of 10 = real signal.

## 5. Organic triallist who didn't sign up (cold email, address from their site)

Use when someone runs a report on their own product but never creates an
account, and you find their email publicly listed. Send within 24h of the run.
Keep it under 10 lines — long emails die unread.

> Subject: you tried OpenCorp — quick q
>
> Hey [name] — Krishav here, founder of OpenCorp. Someone ran a report on
> [their domain] this week — guessing that was you. Got your email from your
> site, hope that's OK.
>
> You're one of the first users, so 3 quick questions:
> 1. How'd you find it?
> 2. Did you see the Hacker News scan, or only the Reddit one?
> 3. What stopped you from signing up?
>
> Happy to draft replies for your 2-3 best threads, free — just say so.
>
> One email only, no drip. Reply reaches me directly.

Q1 = attribution. Q2 separates "didn't see HN" (UX bug) from "didn't want HN"
(fine). Q3 is the gold: first real data on why the unlock gate doesn't pull.
One send, no follow-up — if he doesn't reply, let it go.
