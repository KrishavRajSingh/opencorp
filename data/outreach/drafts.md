# 20 Humanized Cold Outreach Drafts — OpenCorp Day 1

- Sentence 1 mirrors the recipient's specific words / product / post
- Sentence 2 offers opencorp free with the explicit deliverable

Humanization rules applied (per `humanizer` skill):
- No em dashes (use periods, commas, colons, parens)
- No "stands as", "serves as", "boasts a"
- No "additionally", "crucial", "delve"
- No boldface overload, no emojis in message body
- Specific > vague. Real product names, real numbers, real posts.
- Conversational. Direct. No marketing fluff.
