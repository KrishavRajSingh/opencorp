# Surface: Landing (`/`)

## Visitor mode
Persuade — the visitor decides and acts.

## Audience, job, action
- **Audience:** indie hackers and solo founders who have shipped (or are about to ship) a public product page and don't know where their future users are already talking.
- **Job:** find where the next users are already talking, fast — without cold email, without manual subreddit grepping.
- **Action:** paste a product URL → land in the dashboard with the same URL preloaded → run the research agent → get a map of alternatives, Reddit threads, and HN discussions, each with a reason.

## Proof and content on the surface
- Worked example: `filler.live` is the persistent subject on the hero map. It is the only "live" pin and carries the demonstration weight.
- The map shows 8 alternatives, 10 Reddit threads, 12 HN threads = 30 pins distributed across the five regions. Two red threads connect a Reddit thread ↔ competitor (FormPilot) and a Reddit thread ↔ HN thread (the privacy question).
- Founder section: Krishav Raj Singh's first-person statement (brand commitment).
- FAQ: the four product-truth questions from the legacy landing, reframed as a deposition (Q.01–Q.04).
- No invented metrics, no fabricated customer logos, no fake star counts.

## Chosen direction
**The Map Room** — a cartographer's world map where the "continents" are the platforms where conversations happen (REDDIT, HACKER NEWS, GITHUB, TWITTER, INDIHACKERS). The product is a map; the form is the map. Refuses the SaaS landing rut (big H1 + subhead + CTA + feature grid + footer) and the predictable opposite (the Cal.com / Plausible / Supabase OS-launch template).

## Memorable moment
The red thread: a thin, animated line connecting a Reddit thread to a competitor and a HN thread to a privacy thread. It is the visual proof of "we don't just find conversations — we connect them with a reason."

## Unresolved decisions
- Whether the new visual tokens (map-paper, map-ground, map-thread, map-continent) should propagate to the dashboard and auth surfaces, or stay contained to the landing.
- Whether to add a "live" indicator to the dashboard's existing research map (the same vocabulary, different surface).
- Whether the "witness statement" treatment should replace the founder section on the dashboard's empty state.
